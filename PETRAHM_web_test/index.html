ABOUT US

Performance and Value Maximisation

Petra Hospitality Management is an approved white-label hotel operator headquartered in Istanbul, bringing together a multidisciplinary team of experienced hospitality and real estate professionals, focused on driving performance and maximising asset value.

Petra manages 27 hotels — operational and under development — totaling 3,700 keys across three continents. We focus on performance and value maximisation for hotel assets across Turkey, Southeast Europe, the UK and the wider EMEA region.

Our holistic approach to hospitality combines deep industry expertise with adaptability to local market conditions. We share the ambition of generating value through a way of operating based on flexibility, performance and alignment of interest.

OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive Hospitality Solutions

Hotel Management

We manage hotels with flexible operating models across a wide range of hotel segments & brands. Our core focus is to maintain and enhance value through comprehensive operational management.

  • Revenue Management
  • Sales & Marketing
  • Food & Beverage
  • Design, Branding & Communications

  • Human Resources
  • Finance, Accounting & Reporting
  • Pre-opening services
  • Sustainability
  • Development Advisory

Asset Management

We bring end-to-end asset expertise and hard market intelligence to deliver tailored solutions at every stage of your hotel investment.

Key areas
  • Asset value optimisation
  • Management contract oversight
  • Pre-opening & annual budget negotiations
  • FF&E accounting control & expenditure oversight
  • Purchasing efficiency assessments
  • Benchmarking of cost-related KPIs
  • Cash flow & working capital optimisation
  • Competitive set & market analysis
  • CapEx planning & monitoring
  • Evaluation of renovations & ROI

Interim Management

We step in as interim hotel managers to stabilise operations, fix root causes, and value-engineer costs — protecting your brand while lifting efficiency.

Key areas
  • Interim Asset Management
  • Turnaround Management
  • Branding & negotiation of management contract terms
  • Service level contracts & vendor relationships
  • De-flagging

Independent Business Review

We evaluate key parameters for every aspect, from sales and revenue management to staffing and utilities, devising new business plans with estimated cashflow and value potential.

Key areas
  • Concept & market positioning
  • Benchmarking (ADR, occupancy, etc.)
  • Critical review of annual budget
  • Operational performance analysis
  • Revised Business Plan
  • CapEx & Financial Projections
  • NOI vs Debt Service (DSCR, ICR, LTV)
  • Liquidity analysis
  • CapEx funding structures
  • Deconsolidation options (JVs, partial exits)
